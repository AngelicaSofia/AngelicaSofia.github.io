## Talent Accelerator Programme 2025: A Leadership Journey Over Time

The Talent Accelerator Programme (TAP) 2025 at Thales was not a single moment or event, but a semester-long leadership journey designed to support growth with intention, depth, and continuity. Spanning two quarters of the year, the programme combined structured learning, reflection, and human connection to develop not only future leaders, but more self-aware professionals.

From the very beginning, TAP challenged participants to look inward before looking outward. Through personal branding sessions, we were encouraged to reflect on how we show up as leaders, how our values translate into action, and how our impact is perceived by others. These sessions were not about visibility for its own sake, but about alignment — between who we are, what we stand for, and how we lead.

A central element of the journey was the mentorship programme, which created space for honest dialogue, perspective, and guidance. Mentorship throughout TAP was less about answers and more about asking better questions: about direction, responsibility, and long-term growth. This relationship-based learning reinforced the importance of trust and listening as core leadership capabilities.

Another powerful milestone was the [CliftonStrengths assessment](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/home.aspx), which offered a structured way to identify and understand individual strengths. Rather than focusing on gaps or weaknesses, the programme emphasised the value of leading from strengths — recognising what comes naturally, refining it, and intentionally applying it in everyday professional contexts. This approach strengthened confidence while encouraging ownership of personal development.

## Châteauform' Immersive Week: Where Learning Became Experience

The journey culminated in the October 2025 [Châteauform' Immersive Week](https://www.chateauform.com/fr/maison/campus-des-berges-de-seine/), an intense and transformative moment where months of learning converged into lived experience, based in France near Pais. This was where reflection turned into action.

A defining moment of the Immersive Week was the opportunity to engage with [Patrice Caine](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrice_Caine), CEO of Thales, and to hear a clear, big-picture perspective on the organisation’s direction. His contribution helped frame the week not only as a leadership experience, but as a deeper understanding of Thales’ culture, values, and long-term vision. The discussion reinforced a strong sense of alignment across a truly global organisation. Bringing together colleagues from different regions, cultures, and business domains made the experience especially powerful, highlighting both the diversity of perspectives and the shared purpose that connects teams worldwide. This cross-cultural exchange created space to recognise new opportunities for collaboration and future synergies beyond organisational boundaries.

During this week, leadership development moved beyond theory. Through deep conversations, group challenges, and moments of vulnerability, participants were invited to challenge assumptions, share openly, and engage with authenticity. The focus on soft skills — communication, emotional intelligence, courage, and adaptability — became tangible, tested in real-time interactions rather than discussed in abstraction.

What made the [Immersive Week](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/melisa-fernández-schmidt-b83a2b2b_learningeveryday-ineveryway-inonethales-activity-7385008878725271552-EslO/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios&rcm=ACoAACxgzQ8Bq5FHZyEgJi8NtmD4k7flyizdcd0) truly impactful was the human energy. Learning was amplified by trust, by the willingness to listen, and by the diversity of perspectives in the room. Some of the most meaningful insights emerged not during formal sessions, but in informal exchanges — over shared reflections, laughter, and conversations that continued well beyond the agenda.

This balance between structured intensity and genuine connection created a strong sense of community. Colleagues became peers, peers became allies, and in many cases, friendships were formed. The experience reinforced a simple but powerful truth: leadership grows fastest in environments where people feel safe to be real.

## A Journey That Continues

The presence and engagement of senior leaders, speakers, and sponsors throughout the programme sent a clear message: leadership at Thales is built on responsibility, integrity, and long-term trust. TAP 2025 did not aim to create a finished product, but to ignite a continuous leadership journey grounded in self-awareness, collaboration, and purpose.

I leave this experience grateful for the learning, the mentorship, and the relationships built along the way. Most of all, I leave with renewed clarity: leadership is not defined by position, but by how we grow, how we connect, and how intentionally we choose to show up — every day.
